Institutional backing and official validity

Upon graduating from the Specialist Training, you have the full institutional backing of INISCH. Additionally, you can pursue official certifications and endorsements, which are independent and optional.

EC
1375STANDARD
Official certification SEP-CONOCER · Standard EC1375

Issued under the competency standard «Provision of auxiliary services in the traditional and complementary recovery of people's physical and socio-emotional conditions», from Mexico's Secretariat of Public Education through CONOCER. An additional, independent process available on completing the Diploma.

RENAPREGISTRY
Public record Verifiable credential

Your certification is listed in Mexico's National Registry of Competency Standards. Anyone can verify its validity at any time.

◆HAGUE
International validity Hague Apostille

The document can be apostilled under the Hague Convention, giving it legal validity in more than 120 countries.

DC-3STPS
For instructors External Training Agent

On completing the Instructor Certification you may register with Mexico's Ministry of Labour and issue Labour Skills Certificates.

INISCHINSTITUTE
Our own backing Institute certificate

On completing any program you receive your certificate with a verifiable reference number, issued directly by INISCH at no extra cost.

All costs, requirements and certification processes are subject to the conditions set by the corresponding certifying body and may change without notice. INISCH delivers the training; the official certification is issued by the certifying body.
