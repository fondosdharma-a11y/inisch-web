Institutional backing and official validity

Upon graduating from the Specialist Training, you have the full institutional backing of INISCH. Additionally, you can pursue official certifications and endorsements, which are independent and optional.

Official certification

SEP-CONOCER · EC1375

Official Certification from Mexico's Secretariat of Public Education (SEP-CONOCER) under Competency Standard EC1375: "Provision of auxiliary services in the traditional and complementary support of the recovery of people's physical and socio-emotional conditions" (holistic and complementary therapies). An additional process, independent of the training investment, available upon completing the Specialist Diploma.

Public record

RENAP

The SEP-CONOCER certification grants an official credential verifiable in Mexico's National Registry of Competency Standards (RENAP), allowing anyone to verify the validity of the issued document at any time.

International validity

Hague Apostille

Together with the SEP-CONOCER certification, you can process the Hague Apostille, granting international legal validity to the document in more than 120 countries.

For instructors

STPS · DC-3 Certificates

Upon completing the Instructor Certification (Instructor Training), it's possible to pursue registration as an External Training Agent before Mexico's Ministry of Labor (STPS), allowing you to provide workplace training and issue Competency or Labor Skills Certificates (DC-3).

All certification costs, requirements and processes are subject to the conditions established by the corresponding certifying body and may be updated without prior notice by those authorities.
