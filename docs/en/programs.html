You don't have to do them all

Most people who pass through the Institute do only the Intensive Workshop, and with that they resolve what they came for. That's perfectly fine: that program doesn't exist to sell another one.

The Diploma is for those who decide to make this a profession — a different thing, a different commitment. And the Instructor Certification does require the two previous programs, because teaching demands having walked and applied it. It is the only real prerequisite that exists, and it is there out of responsibility.
