Professional training

Diploma and Certification as Self-Knowledge Specialist

Eight months to turn understanding into a profession. A training for those who want to guide others with methodology, not intuition.

⚠
This program has a prerequisite

To enter, you must have completed the Intensive Workshop of the Holographic Code System.

This is not a commercial barrier. Here you learn to guide another person's process, and that requires having walked it yourself first. No one can guide another to where they have not arrived first.

A different program, a different audience

This training is not «the next level» of anything. It is a distinct program for a distinct person: someone who no longer comes only to resolve their own situation, but who wants to dedicate themselves to guiding other people's processes.

It has two clear moments. First you work on yourself in depth; then you learn to hold someone else's process, with supervised practice. In that order, and for a reason: no one can guide another to where they have not arrived first.

What it includes

The structure of the work

Theoretical foundation

Quantum Physics, Metaphysics, Lacanian and Jungian Psychology, and the Hermetic Laws, integrated into a single map.

Methodological development

How a session is conducted, what to ask and what not to, and how to hold what emerges.

Supervised practice

An intensive in-person weekend dedicated to applying the method and receiving direct feedback.

Your own process

Before guiding, you walk it. A good part of these eight months is work on your own film.

It's for you if…

  • You want to guide processes as a profession, not a hobby
  • You already work with people and want a structured methodology
  • You're looking for officially recognized certification
  • You can sustain a weekly session for eight months

Perhaps not yet if…

  • What you want is to resolve something of your own — the Intensive Workshop is for that
  • You don't have sustained weekly availability
  • You expect to practise immediately without prior supervised work
