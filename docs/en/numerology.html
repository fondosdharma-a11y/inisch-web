Holographic Numerology

Workshops and consultations that use numerology as a tool for exploration and self-knowledge, allowing you to identify patterns, talents, challenges, cycles and aspects related to your personal purpose.

Within the framework of the Holographic Code System, numerology is approached as a symbolic language that helps read life patterns — not as a fixed prediction of the future. It's offered both as a group workshop and as an individual consultation.
