The principle that holds it all

There is one sentence that runs through every program, every consultation and every journey of the Institute. It is not a marketing slogan: it is a working criterion.

No one can guide another to where they have not arrived first.

The whole structure of the System follows from it: first you walk your own process, and only then do you learn to guide. There is no possible shortcut.
