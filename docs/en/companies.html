For organizations

Wellbeing and self-knowledge for teams

Programs for companies that want to address burnout, communication and workplace climate at the root — with the option of issuing DC-3 certificates recognized by Mexico's Ministry of Labour (STPS).

The problem most workshops don't solve

Most corporate wellbeing programs work on symptoms: stress management techniques, team-building activities, motivational talks. They work for a few days, and then the team returns to exactly the same pattern.

The Holographic Code System starts somewhere else: a team's conflicts are rarely about the topic being discussed. They are patterns of control, expectation and attachment that each person brings from their own history and projects onto work. When that becomes conscious, the dynamic shifts on its own.

What we address

Topics that apply directly to the workplace

Control and delegation

Why certain profiles find it impossible to let go of tasks, and what sits underneath that need to supervise everything.

Expectations and frustration

The gap between what we expect from others and what actually happens — the main source of conflict between departments.

Judgment and criticism

How the habit of evaluating others erodes collaboration, and what sustains it.

Conscious communication

Listening without preparing your reply. Speaking without needing to be right.

Managing burnout

Fine attention and presence practices applied to high-demand workloads.

Team dynamics

Recognizing the characters each person repeats and how they entangle within a working group.

Official backing

DC-3 certificates with the STPS

Our instructors can register as External Training Agents with Mexico's Ministry of Labour and Social Welfare (STPS), which allows issuing DC-3 Labour Competency Certificates to participants.

For your company this means the training doesn't only affect workplace climate — it also contributes to your training compliance record.

Issuing DC-3 certificates and any official registration or procedure is subject to the requirements set by the competent authority. It is worth confirming availability for your specific program before contracting it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum group size?

It depends on the format. Experiential days work best with small groups because they include work in pairs and triads; talks accommodate large audiences. We define it together based on your goal.

Can it be delivered online?

Yes. We work both in person and online. Experiential dynamics gain a lot from being in person, but they adapt to remote formats when the team is distributed.

Is this therapy?

No. It is an educational and self-knowledge process. It does not constitute psychological diagnosis or treatment and does not replace care from a mental health professional. If someone needs that kind of support during the process, we say so clearly.

How is it priced?

Each program is built to measure based on participant count, format, duration, delivery mode and whether certificates are required. Write to us and we'll prepare a proposal.
