The origin of the Holographic Code System

A methodology born from direct experience, not theory.

Isabel Elizalde

The Holographic Code System was created by Isabel Elizalde, who throughout her life had clairvoyant and extrasensory experiences that led her to develop this self-knowledge methodology. Through her own process of personal transformation, she designed a system that logically and demonstrably integrates exact sciences, psychological currents and ancestral wisdom.

Since then, she has dedicated her work to sharing these teachings so others can discover the patterns of their unconscious, understand the origin of their life patterns, and train their minds toward greater well-being.

The Holographic Code System

It logically and demonstrably integrates disciplines such as Quantum Physics, Metaphysics, Lacanian Psychology, Jungian Psychology and Hermetic Laws. Its central premise is that the outer environment is, to a large extent, a reflection of the inner world: learning to observe that reflection consciously is the first step toward real transformation.

"No one can guide another to where they have not gone themselves."

That is why every level of training at INISCH begins with a process of deep personal immersion, before taking the step toward the professional practice of guiding others.

INISCH

The International Institute of the Holographic Code System exists to give formal structure to this methodology: a path of exploration, learning and personal transformation through training, specialized guidance and experiences designed to deepen self-knowledge.
