Self-knowledge program

Intensive Workshop of the Holographic Code System

Two days to understand why your life repeats the way it does. A process complete in itself: it begins here and it can end here.

What you come to resolve

Very different people arrive, but almost everyone brings the same underlying feeling: I already tried to change this and it happened again. Another relationship with the same ending. Another job with the same conflict. The same argument with a different person.

The Holographic Code System starts from a concrete idea: that isn't bad luck or coincidence. It's a film that was edited in your mind between ages 2 and 10, and that today projects through your eyes onto everything you live.

What we work on

The five movements

The conscious observer

Stop feeling like a victim of circumstance and take the place of the one who observes their own reality.

Your film

How it formed, and how to recognize its scenes when they are repeating today.

Control, expectation, attachment

The three roots of everyday suffering, identified one by one.

Authentic forgiveness

Not as forgetting or justifying, but as the understanding that appears on seeing the other person's story.

Real Love

Understanding, Compassion and Kindness as the three components that modify the film.

Experiential work

These are not lectures. They are dynamics in pairs and triads where you recognize yourself by listening to others.

It's for you if…

  • You feel a pattern repeating and can't quite name it
  • You've never done self-knowledge work before
  • You've been in therapy for years and want another lens
  • You want to understand your story without staying in complaint

It's not for you if…

  • You're in a crisis that needs clinical attention now
  • You're looking for a diagnosis or psychological treatment
  • You expect someone to tell you what to do with your life
  • You don't want to look at your own childhood

This is a self-knowledge process, formative in nature. It does not constitute psychological diagnosis or treatment and does not replace care from a mental health professional.

It can end here

Many people do the Intensive Workshop and need nothing more. It is not a step towards something else and it commits you to nothing: it is a complete process that closes in itself. If you later want to go deeper, other programs exist — but that is your decision, and one for another moment.
