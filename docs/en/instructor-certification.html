Advanced professional program

Certification as SCH Instructor

120 hours for those who already guide processes and now want to teach, lead groups and develop their own self-knowledge processes.

⚠
This program does have a prerequisite

To enter, you must have completed the Intensive Workshop and the Specialist Diploma.

This is not a commercial barrier: it is the only responsible approach. Teaching this methodology requires having walked it and having applied it with real people. No one can guide another to where they have not arrived first — and that applies above all to whoever is going to train others.

From guiding to teaching

Accompanying one person and leading a group are different crafts. This program develops the second: adult pedagogy, group dynamics, public speaking, and the ability to adapt the System to audiences with scientific, rational or spiritual perspectives.

Here you stop reacting to the structure and begin reorganizing it with intention, from the role of facilitator.

Curriculum

Three modules

Module 1

The Root of Total Knowledge

Integration of Psychology, Energetics and Quantum Physics with the Lacanian registers and the Holographic Code, as a framework for analysing existential processes.

Module 2

Pedagogy of the Future and Leadership

Instructional design for adults, neuroscience applied to learning, group facilitation and interpersonal communication.

Module 3

The Instructor: Marketing and AI

Professional presence in the digital environment, content creation and use of tools such as ChatGPT, Gamma, Canva and Zoom.
