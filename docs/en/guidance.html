Specialized Guidance

Individual consultations with our Self-Knowledge Specialists, conceived as spaces for listening, reflection and understanding to accompany different personal development processes.

"The outer environment is simply a reflection of the inner world."

Consultations are individual and are scheduled directly through WhatsApp with the INISCH team, who will connect you with the Specialist best suited to your process.
