Specialist Training in Self-Knowledge

A comprehensive training program in the Holographic Code System, oriented toward developing competencies to guide and facilitate self-knowledge processes. With optional certification before SEP-CONOCER.

"You live the process first. Then you learn to facilitate it."

Stage 1

Awakening — The Awakening

Personal Interiorization Guide of the Holographic Code System

This stage helps a person stop perceiving themselves as a victim of circumstances and take on the role of conscious observer of their own reality. It helps you understand that the outer environment is simply a reflection of the inner world. Ideal for those seeking to understand the origin of their life patterns.

By the end of this stage you will be able to:

  • Expand your thought structure to incorporate new perspectives in understanding your personal experiences.
  • Identify the roots of suffering by recognizing patterns of thought, emotion and behavior.
  • Develop emotional autonomy through the conscious management of expectations, attachments and codependency dynamics.
  • Understand how conscious and unconscious processes work and how they influence perception.
  • Recognize the relationship between thoughts, emotions and behaviors.
  • Integrate forgiveness as a process of understanding, release and personal transformation.
  • Transform your personal perception from the concept of Real Love: understanding, compassion and kindness.

Requirements

  • Commitment to the process of transformation and personal development.
  • Open program, no prior experience needed.
  • No prior studies, knowledge or training required.

Stage 2

Mastery — The Encounter With Yourself

Certification as a Self-Knowledge Specialist of the Holographic Code System

The training unfolds in two clearly differentiated and consecutive moments, each with a specific purpose within the participant's preparation process.

I transform

Personal Mastery: the participant works on themselves. Focused exclusively on self-knowledge, introspection and personal transformation. Can be taken independently, without requiring continuation toward Specialist training.

I guide

Specialization Training: the participant learns to guide others. Theoretical foundations, analysis tools, application protocols and communication skills, with practice, simulations and supervision.

Upon graduating, the student has the full institutional backing of INISCH. Additionally, they may opt for the Official SEP-CONOCER Certification under Competency Standard EC1375, with a credential verifiable at RENAP, as well as the Hague Apostille, with legal validity in more than 120 countries.

Training structure

  • Total duration: 8 months · 128 hours of training.
  • Weekly online session: 4 hours, from 9:00 am to 1:00 pm.
  • Intensive in-person practice: one weekend dedicated to application and supervision.
  • Combines theoretical foundation, methodological development, practice and supervision.

Stage 3

Instructor Training

From personal transformation to professional development

Prepares Self-Knowledge Specialists to teach, facilitate training processes, lead groups and develop their own educational projects under the SCH methodology, with an interdisciplinary theoretical foundation and pedagogical, communication and professional tools.

Requirements

  • Have successfully completed Stage 1 and Stage 2.
  • Have prior experience applying the SCH methodology.
  • Demonstrate commitment to personal, professional and ethical development.
  • Willingness to take on responsibilities as a facilitator, instructor and leader of training processes.
  • Openness to integrate new knowledge, perspectives and pedagogical tools.

Curriculum · 3 modules

  • Module 1 — The Root of Total Knowledge: interdisciplinary integration of Psychology, Energetics and Quantum Physics with Lacanian frameworks and the Holographic Code.
  • Module 2 — Pedagogy of the Future and Leadership: an inclusive and ethical facilitator role, instructional planning for adults, neuroscience applied to learning, group management.
  • Module 3 — The Instructor, Marketing and Artificial Intelligence: digital positioning, persuasive marketing and communication, strategic use of AI (ChatGPT, Gamma) and tools like Canva and Zoom.

Frequently asked questions about the program

Do I need prior knowledge to get started?

No. Stage 1 is open to the general public and requires no prior studies, knowledge or training. Only commitment to the process of personal transformation is asked.

Do I have to complete all 3 stages?

No. Stage 1 can be taken independently. Even the first half of Stage 2 (Personal Mastery) can be taken as a personal development process without continuing toward Specialist certification.

Is the SEP-CONOCER certification mandatory?

No, it's optional and independent of the training cost. You can complete Stage 2 with INISCH's institutional backing and decide later whether to pursue official certification.

Are classes in-person or online?

It depends on the stage. Stage 1 is offered in both formats. Stages 2 and 3 combine weekly online sessions with an intensive in-person practice weekend.

Can I pay in monthly installments?

Yes. Stages 2 and 3 have an enrollment-plus-monthly-payments scheme, detailed for each stage above.

Does this replace psychological therapy?

No. Our programs are self-knowledge and personal development processes; they do not replace professional medical, psychological or psychiatric treatment. If you are going through a mental health crisis, we recommend seeking specialized help.

How do I access my classes after enrolling?

Through the Student Portal, where you'll find your lessons, your progress and your certificate upon completing each stage.
