Frequently asked questions

The questions we get most, answered plainly. If yours isn't here, write to us.

Before you start

Do I need any prior knowledge for the Intensive Workshop?

No. The Intensive Workshop is designed as the entry point and requires no previous training of any kind. People arrive who have never done self-knowledge work, and people who have been in therapy for years.

Is this psychological therapy?

No. It is a self-knowledge process, formative in nature. It does not constitute psychological diagnosis or treatment and does not replace care from a mental health professional. If someone needs that kind of support during the process, we say so clearly.

Is it a religion? Do I have to believe in anything?

No. The System does not ask you to adopt any belief. The work is done by observing your own experience. Everyone keeps their own beliefs, whatever they are.

I'm going through a very hard time. Is this the right moment?

It depends. The work stirs up personal material, and there are moments when an individual space is more appropriate than a group. Write to us and we'll look at it with you honestly. If you are in crisis, professional support comes first.

Is there a minimum age?

The programs are designed for adults. If you have a question about a particular case, get in touch.

About the programs

How long does each program last?

The Intensive Workshop is 2 days with 16 hours of work. The Specialist Diploma runs 8 months and totals 128 hours. The Instructor Certification is 120 hours.

Do I have to take all three programs?

No, and this matters: most people do only the Intensive Workshop and with that they resolve what they came for. That program closes in itself and requires nothing beforehand. The other two are for those who want to make this a profession, and they do have prerequisites: the Diploma requires having completed the Intensive Workshop, and the Instructor Certification requires both the Workshop and the Diploma. It isn't a sales tactic: to guide someone else's process you must have walked it yourself first.

Can I take it online?

The Diploma combines a weekly 4-hour online session with in-person weekend gatherings. The experiential dynamics gain a great deal in person, but we adapt formats case by case.

What if I miss a session?

Write to us as soon as you know you can't attend and we'll look at how to make it up. The specific conditions for make-up sessions and material access are part of your enrollment terms.

How many people are in each group?

Groups are kept small because the work includes dynamics in pairs and triads. That's why places are limited.

Certifications

Is the SEP-CONOCER certification included?

No, it is optional and has a separate cost. For founding students it is $6,062 MXN, broken down into alignment ($3,693) and certificate issuance ($2,369).

What is the EC1375 standard?

It is the competency standard under which the certification is issued. On obtaining it, your credential is listed in Mexico's RENAP, the national registry of certified individuals.

Is it valid outside Mexico?

The certification can be apostilled under the Hague Convention, which facilitates recognition in more than 120 countries. Each country decides how it recognizes foreign documents, so it's worth confirming for your specific case.

Can I teach in companies once I finish?

Instructors trained in the Instructor Certification may register as an External Training Agent with Mexico's Ministry of Labour (STPS), which allows issuing DC-3 certificates. Registration and its requirements depend on the authority.

Investment and payment

How much does each program cost?

Intensive Workshop: $8,500 MXN (approx. $500 USD). Specialist Diploma: $36,000 MXN, with $6,000 enrollment and 6 monthly payments of $5,000. Instructor Certification for founding members: $1,999 enrollment and 9 monthly payments of $3,963.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. The Diploma and the Instructor Certification are paid with an initial enrollment plus monthly payments.

The Diploma lasts 8 months. Why only 6 monthly payments?

Because payment isn't spread across the whole program. You pay the $6,000 MXN enrollment when you enroll and then 6 monthly payments of $5,000 MXN, which adds up to exactly $36,000 MXN. The final two months of training carry no payment.

What does the Instructor Certification cost in total?

On the regular scheme, $57,800 MXN ($8,000 enrollment plus 6 monthly payments of $8,300). At the founding price, $37,666 MXN ($1,999 enrollment plus 9 monthly payments of $3,963). The founding scheme is spread across 9 months instead of 6.

How can I pay?

You can pay online by card or in cash at OXXO through the payment buttons on each program page. You'll receive email confirmation.

Are the dollar prices fixed?

No. Amounts in dollars are indicative and may vary with the daily exchange rate. The reference price is the one in Mexican pesos.

Do you issue invoices?

Yes. Let us know when you enroll so we can include your tax details.

Can I cancel and get a refund?

Cancellation and refund conditions are part of the enrollment terms. Contact us before enrolling if you'd like to review them in detail.

The student portal and materials

Do I get access to an online portal?

Yes. Once your enrollment is confirmed you receive access to the student portal, with your lessons, your private notes and the System's working tools.

Is what I write in the portal private?

Completely. My Film, the Journal, the Practice log and your notes are strictly private: no instructor, administrator or fellow student can see them. The database is configured so that each account can only reach its own records.

Can I download what I write?

Yes. From your profile you can export everything you've written at any time.
