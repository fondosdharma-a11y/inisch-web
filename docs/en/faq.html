Frequently Asked Questions

The most common questions before enrolling in an INISCH program.

Do I need prior knowledge to get started?

No. Stage 1 (Awakening) is open to the general public and does not require prior studies, knowledge or training. Only commitment to the process of personal transformation is asked.

Do I have to complete all 3 stages to take Stage 1?

No. Stage 1 can be taken independently. Even Stage 2 (Personal Mastery, the first half) can be taken as a personal development process without needing to continue toward Specialist certification.

Is the SEP-CONOCER certification mandatory?

No, it's optional and independent of the training cost. You can complete Stage 2 with INISCH's institutional backing and decide afterward whether you want to pursue the official certification.

Are classes in-person or online?

It depends on the stage. Stage 1 is offered in both in-person and online format. Stages 2 and 3 combine weekly online sessions with an intensive in-person practice weekend.

Can I pay in monthly installments?

Yes. Stages 2 and 3 have an enrollment-plus-monthly-payments scheme. See the exact detail for each stage on the Program page.

How do I access my classes after enrolling?

Through the Student Portal, where you'll find your lessons, your progress and your certificate upon completing each stage.

Can I become a certified instructor of the Holographic Code System?

Yes, through Stage 3: Instructor Training, which requires having completed Stages 1 and 2. Upon finishing it, you may optionally pursue registration as an External Training Agent before Mexico's STPS.
